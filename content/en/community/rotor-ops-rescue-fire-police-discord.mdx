## What Is RotorOps: Rescue, Fire & Police?

Developed by Veltsim_Officiel, *RotorOps - Rescue & Fire & Police* is an immersive multiplayer vehicle simulator hosted on the [Roblox platform](https://www.roblox.com). Unlike arcade flight titles, this simulation places an intense emphasis on authentic protocols, technical piloting, complex pre-hospital trauma care, aerial wildland firefighting, and police tactical reconnaissance.

Rather than simply flying from waypoint to waypoint, players assume specialized roles across three distinct emergency service branches:

1. **Emergency Medical Services (EMS / SAR):** Pilot multi-role rescue helicopters, deploy winches onto steep terrain, land in narrow urban streets, and treat patients using realistic medical kits.
2. **Aerial Firefighting:** Pilot bucket-equipped helicopters and massive air tankers to drop water fronts, construct firebreaks, and protect structures from dynamic, wind-driven blazes.
3. **Law Enforcement (Gendarmerie / Police):** Conduct night patrols, track fleeing suspects or missing hikers with thermal forward-looking infrared (FLIR) cameras, and maintain tactical command from above.

Because every operation rewards tight collaboration, linking up via the **rotor ops rescue fire police discord** allows pilots and ground specialists to share missions, maximize payouts, and execute multi-craft operations that solo players simply cannot pull off.

## Emergency Branches and Role Mechanics

Every emergency discipline in the game features specialized equipment and mechanics designed to test both reaction time and protocol knowledge.

### EMS & Search and Rescue Protocols

In medical emergencies, simply loading a casualty into the cabin is not an option. Patients cannot be hoisted or moved until you stabilize their injuries on-site. Player reports highlight that treating critical trauma demands strict prioritization:

| Trauma Equipment | Clinical Purpose | In-Game Trigger / Use Case |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cervical Collar** | C-spine stabilization | Falls, vehicle rollovers, blunt impact |
| **Traction / Rigid Splint** | Fracture immobilization | Broken limbs, severe crush injuries |
| **Combat Tourniquet** | Severe hemorrhage control | Arterial bleeds, traumatic amputations |
| **Intubation Probe** | Securing compromised airway | Unconscious patients with hypoxia |
| **Mechanical Respirator** | Assisted ventilation | Respiratory failure, smoke inhalation |

Crew members must open the casualty's medical file, diagnose life threats, and administer the proper intervention before the winch hoist hook can even be secured.

### Aerial Firefighting Tactics

Wildfires in RotorOps feature wind propagation mechanics. If a crew fails to drop retardant or water ahead of the flame front, the fire jumps gaps and destroys entire residential sectors.

```
Wind Direction: [ West to East ]
Strategy: Drop water dams AHEAD of the front, not behind the burned ash.
Aircraft Mix: 1x Scout Spotter (FLIR) + 2x Water Bombers (CL-415 / Dash 8)
```

Pilots operating heavy scooper aircraft such as the Canadair CL-415 or the Dash 8 air tanker must coordinate approach vectors carefully to avoid mid-air collisions over smoky drop zones.

### Why won't my injured patient hoist up to the helicopter?
RotorOps requires full medical stabilization before a patient can be winched. Check the patient's medical file to ensure all required items—such as a cervical collar, splints, tourniquets, or intubation tubes—have been successfully administered.

### What is the most effective aircraft for wildfire containment?

### Can I earn more currency by flying in squads?
