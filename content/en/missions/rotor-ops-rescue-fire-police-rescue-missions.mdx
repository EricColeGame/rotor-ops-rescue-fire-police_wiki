## Step-by-Step Guide: SAMU Medical Rescue Missions

Medical calls are among the most frequent tasks in the game. In medical sorties, your objective is not merely landing next to an injured NPC; you must properly stabilize the patient before initiating transport to a regional medical center.

```
Accept Mission Dispatch ➔ Navigate to Waypoint ➔ Evaluate LZ / Hover Position 
➔ Deploy Crew/Hoist ➔ Administer Medical Aid ➔ Hoist Patient Onboard ➔ Hospital Medevac
```

### Medical Extraction Workflow

Executing a flawless medical extraction involves four distinct phases that require seamless coordination between the pilot maintaining a steady hover and the paramedic managing gear on the ground.

| Phase | Pilot Responsibility | Crew / Paramedic Responsibility | Common Failure Point |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **1. Inbound & Survey** | Follow waypoint compass, check terrain gradient, spot wires | Prep medical kit UI, monitor altimeter callouts | Crashing into unseen treelines or cables |
| **2. Extraction Setup** | Establish stabilized hover or touch down safely | Deploy winch line or disembark on ground | Drifting out of hoist range during cable drop |
| **3. Patient Care** | Maintain collective stability against wind | Interact with patient, bandage, apply splints | Rushing the hoist before patient is stabilized |
| **4. Medevac Return** | Navigate to marked hospital helipad, smooth flare | Monitor patient vitals in transit | Hard landings damaging airframe at hospital |

When performing mountain operations during complex **rotor ops rescue fire police rescue missions**, ground slopes often prevent a standard three-point touchdown. Pilots must master the partial hover—resting one skid on the slope while applying sufficient collective to keep the rotor disc level while the medic works.

## Conquering Wildfire Operations: Aerial Firefighting

Wildfire calls introduce dynamic environmental threats. Fires in this simulation spread realistically, influenced by simulated wind vectors and changing fuel densities across the terrain. 

Flying water-bombing sorties requires precise calculation of altitude, airspeeds, and drop delays. Flying too fast causes your water payload to disperse harmlessly in the updraft, while dropping from too high allows heat thermals to vaporize the drop before it smothers the flame front.

| Step Number | Tactical Objective | Flight Technique | Critical Consideration |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Step 1** | Water Source Refill | Hover-dip bucket in lakes/ocean or land at retardant base | Avoid overfilling bucket beyond rotor lift capacity |
| **Step 2** | Approach Vector | Fly perpendicular or diagonally across the wind front | Entering direct downwind runs causes severe downdrafts |
| **Step 3** | Drop Execution | Reduce speed to 40–60 knots at 50–100 feet AGL | Trigger release right before leading edge of flames |
| **Step 4** | Egress and Turn | Apply maximum collective, bank clear of smoke plumes | Smoke reduces cockpit visibility and engine efficiency |

### Wind Management Tactics

To halt a rapidly advancing blaze, fire teams should target the head of the fire rather than trailing ashes. Coordinating multiple aircraft—such as having an EC145 douse perimeter spot fires while fixed-wing platforms drop heavy retardant lines across the main vector—is the fastest way to clear high-difficulty calls.

## Gendarmerie Operations: Police Surveillance & Search

Gendarmerie sorties reward situational awareness, steady hover control, and smart utilization of avionics. Police missions generally revolve around locating lost hikers in remote wilderness, tracking fleeing suspects through highway networks, or providing persistent illumination for ground teams.

### Core Avionics and Equipment

- **FLIR (Forward Looking Infrared):** Essential for picking out human heat signatures hidden beneath dense jungle canopies or rocky terrain. Hot targets show up bright white or black depending on the polarity mode.
- **Night Vision Systems (NVG):** Best used during low-light night flights to spot ridgelines and high-voltage transmission towers that FLIR narrow-aperture cameras might miss.
- **TrakkaBeam / Searchlight:** Slaved to the crew member's camera or gimbal stick, illuminating targets for ground units or SAMU landing zones.

## Maximizing Credits and Common Mistakes to Avoid

Earning enough credits to expand your hangar fleet requires disciplined sorties and avoiding costly airframe repairs. Here are the core errors that set pilots back:

### Major Flight and Mission Mistakes

- **Vortex Ring State (VRS):** Descending too rapidly (greater than 500 ft/min) while at near-zero forward airspeed causes your helicopter to settle into its own downwash. If your aircraft begins violently shaking and sinking, push the cyclic forward to gain forward airspeed and exit the dirty air column.
- **Skipping Medical Steps:** Attempting to hoist an injured NPC before completing all treatment prompts will cancel the pick-up sequence, forcing your crew to restart the care timer.
- **Overloading Rotor Discs in Firefighting:** Trying to climb vertically out of a mountain reservoir with a full water bucket often over-torques your engines. Always build forward airspeed along the surface of the lake before initiating a steep climb.
- **Flying Solo on Complex Calls:** Taking on challenging **rotor ops rescue fire police rescue missions** without crew means you have to juggle flying, camera spotting, and winching entirely on your own. Take advantage of public servers and fly as a dedicated duo whenever possible.

By mastering the unique demands of each department, understanding your aircraft's flight envelope, and working seamlessly with your crew, you will excel in every emergency response you undertake.

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### What is the fastest way to earn credits in RotorOps?
The fastest way to accumulate credits is by running consecutive SAMU medical rescues with a two-person crew. Having a dedicated pilot remain in a hover while a paramedic handles on-site treatment significantly reduces mission completion times compared to solo attempts.

### How do I recover from settling with power or Vortex Ring State (VRS)?
If your helicopter begins dropping uncontrollably while hovering or descending slowly, do not merely pull up on the collective. Push your nose forward to establish forward translational lift (airspeed) out of the turbulent air bubble, and only apply collective once positive airflow is re-established.

### What aircraft should I unlock first in rotor ops rescue fire police rescue missions?
Most experienced players recommend mastering the default EC145 Civil Security helicopter before spending currency. Once you have saved around 160,000 credits, buy the EC135, which provides the best balance of speed, handling, and agility across all medical and police assignments.

### Can I run aerial firefighting missions with the starter helicopters?
Yes. Starter multi-role helicopters such as the EC145 can be outfitted with water buckets for fire calls. While they carry smaller payloads than dedicated fixed-wing water bombers like the CL-415, their ability to hover-refill from compact mountain ponds makes them highly versatile for precision spot-fire suppression.
